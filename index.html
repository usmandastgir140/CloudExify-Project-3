Our Studio

Fifteen years of quiet precision

Usman Dental opened in 2011 with a single idea: dental care should feel considered, not clinical. Every suite is private, every specialist is board-certified in their discipline, and every plan is built around the patient in the chair — not a script.

  • Private treatment suites — no shared bays, no overlapping appointments
  • Specialist-led care across six disciplines, not generalist rotation
  • Digital scanning and imaging on every visit, no guesswork
  • Transparent, itemized treatment plans before any work begins
